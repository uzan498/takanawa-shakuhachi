MINATO CITY, TOKYO　TOZAN-RYU SHAKUHACHI

No fees.
Instruments lent free.

A shakuhachi class run by Minato City as a community programme. You do not need to read music, and you do not need an instrument. Come as you are.

Takanawa Ikiiki Plaza
Shinmei Children and Youth Plaza

About the class　↓
01

The class

Held three times a month at Takanawa Ikiiki Plaza in Minato City.

Venue
Takanawa Ikiiki Plaza, meeting room
3-18-15 Takanawa, Minato City, Tokyo
Access
8 min walk from Shinagawa or Sengakuji Station
7 min from Takanawa Gateway Station
Schedule
Three times a month (weekday evenings, weekend mornings and afternoons)
Eligibility
Residents of Minato City, or those who work or study there, aged 10 and over
Fee
Free
Instrument
Lent free of charge. You may bring your own if you have one
Repertoire
Classical honkyoku and sankyoku ensemble, as well as popular songs and bossa nova. Tozan school certification is available on request
Language
Lessons are conducted in Japanese. Please contact us in advance if you would like to attend
5 min

Many people produce their first note within five minutes.

¥0

No tuition, no instrument rental, no materials fee.

3×/month

Come every session, or once a month. Either is fine.

02

Your first visit

Nothing to bring. Ordinary clothes are fine.

  1. 01
    Get in touchSend an email saying you would like to join the shakuhachi class. We will let you know the dates.
  2. 02
    Come empty-handedThe venue is Takanawa Ikiiki Plaza. An instrument will be lent to you.
  3. 03
    Learn how to hold itHow to hold the flute, and where to place your lips. We take our time here.
  4. 04
    Make a soundOften within five minutes. You should get at least one note on the first day.
  5. 05
    Choose what to play nextWe decide together what you would like to work on.
